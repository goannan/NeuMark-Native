VALL-E Native Watermarking

Robust In-Model Latent Audio Watermarking for Generative Neural Speech Synthesis

Author: Annan Wu
Code (NeuMark-Native)
Abstract & Overview

Generative speech synthesis models (such as neural codec language models like VALL-E) achieve unprecedented zero-shot voice cloning capabilities, posing substantial safety and copyright traceability challenges. Traditional post-processing audio watermarks (e.g., AudioSeal, WavMark) degrade perceptual speech quality or are vulnerable to neural codec compression and acoustic perturbations.

In this project, we propose VALL-E Native Latent Watermarking, embedding covert, multi-bit cryptographic watermark representations directly into the discrete latent acoustic space and residual quantization representations during generative synthesis. By jointly conditioning on native speech tokens and differentiable acoustic distortion channels, our approach preserves pristine acoustic fidelity and natural prosody while guaranteeing robust watermark extraction across diverse acoustic environments.
